Production Process

From initial vision to final delivery — a clear workflow for every project.

Pre-Production Production Post-Production Delivery
Phase 1

Pre-Production

1 Vision

Vision from event leader or Design Director

2 Kickoff

Design Director presents vision to Design Team — mood board, timeline, etc.

3 Concepts

Designers create and present concepts for Core Design

4 Review

Design Director chooses options to present to Event Leader

5 Decision

Event Leader selects an option or requests revisions, landing on a Core Design

6 Meeting

Brainstorming with Design Team based on the Core Design

7 Meeting

Creative Lead Team reviews brainstorming ideas during weekly touchpoint

8 Approval

SIT reviews curated Design Team ideas and approves Design Plan

Moves to Production
Phase 2

Production

1 Assign

Projects assigned to Designers and Editors

2 Meeting

Specific project meetings with Designers and Editors for clarity and detail planning

3 Build

Designers and Editors work through and complete assigned projects and tasks

4 Meeting

Event Leader reviews overall progress and completed assignments

↩ May follow to Post-Production 1 — Test folder review
5 Revisions

Project revisions based on feedback

↩ May return from Post-Production 2 — Screen testing
6 Meeting

Near-final versions presented to Event Leader on screen or hard copy (if possible)

7 Proofing

Proofing

8 Final

Design / Edit Final

→ May proceed to Post-Production 3 — Print + Screens
Moves to Post-Production
Phase 3

Post-Production

1 Screens

Test folder created and shared with Production Content Lead

↩ May originate from Production 4 — Event Leader Review
2 Screens

Early versions tested on screens and/or reviewed for TC

↩ May return to Production 5 — Project revisions
3 Print + Screens

Print: Collateral ordered

Screens: Final export placed in Export folder

→ Follows from Production 8 — Final
4 Print + Screens

Print: Order inspected and approved

Screens: File moved to Weekend & Event folder

Moves to Delivery
Phase 4

Delivery

1 Print + Screens

Print: Collateral delivered to appropriate team leader

Screens: Final tech review and approval

✓

Project Complete
